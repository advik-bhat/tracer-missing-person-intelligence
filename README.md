# TRACER

### AI-Assisted Missing Person Intelligence & Rescue Platform

> **From scattered leads → prioritized intelligence.**

TRACER is an AI-assisted investigator decision-support platform designed to help organize missing-person information, analyze incoming sightings, prioritize investigative leads, and identify potential patterns across multiple reports.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://tracer.bolt.host

---

## 🎯 Problem

Missing-person investigations can generate large amounts of fragmented information:

- Sightings from different locations
- Photographs and descriptions
- Timestamps
- Witness reports
- Physical attributes
- Multiple overlapping leads

The challenge isn't simply storing this information.

The challenge is determining **which leads deserve attention first — and why.**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

TRACER brings these pieces of information into a single investigation workflow.

The system:

1. Creates and manages missing-person cases
2. Collects incoming sightings and reports
3. Extracts relevant attributes from unstructured information
4. Compares sightings against the case profile
5. Calculates a transparent lead-priority score
6. Correlates sightings across time and location
7. Highlights potentially meaningful patterns
8. Allows investigators to review the underlying evidence and decide the next action

TRACER is designed to **assist investigators, not replace them.**

---

## 🧭 Trail Convergence

Our key differentiator is **Trail Convergence**.

Individual sightings may not provide much information on their own. However, multiple sightings can become more meaningful when they show:

- Temporal proximity
- Geographic proximity
- Overlapping attributes
- A plausible sequence between locations

TRACER combines these signals to identify a **potential movement corridor**.

For example:

> 08:52 — Central Station  
> 09:03 — Metro Junction  
> 09:14 — Bus Terminal

Instead of treating these as three isolated reports, TRACER can surface their potential correlation for investigator review.

**Important:** Trail Convergence is an investigative signal, not proof of identity or a confirmed route.

> **We're not trying to manufacture certainty. We're trying to surface useful patterns.**
