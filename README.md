# TRACER

### AI-Assisted Missing Person Intelligence & Rescue Platform

> From scattered leads → prioritized intelligence.

TRACER is an AI-assisted investigation platform designed to help authorized investigators organize fragmented missing-person information, prioritize incoming leads, and identify potential correlations between sightings.

Built for **DOMINION 2026 — Missing Persons & Rescue Track**.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Missing-person investigations can generate information from many different sources:

- Witness reports
- Photographs
- Physical descriptions
- Sightings
- Locations
- Timestamps
- Other contextual information

The challenge isn't always a lack of information — it's **connecting and prioritizing that information quickly**.

Investigators may have multiple leads but limited time to determine which ones deserve immediate attention.

### TRACER asks:

> **Given all available leads, which ones deserve investigator attention first — and why?**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

TRACER transforms fragmented reports into structured and explainable investigative intelligence.

The platform:

1. Organizes missing-person cases
2. Receives and structures new sightings
3. Uses AI to extract relevant attributes from reports
4. Compares incoming information with existing case data
5. Calculates a transparent lead-priority score
6. Correlates sightings using time, location, and shared attributes
7. Highlights potential movement corridors
8. Keeps the investigator responsible for the final decision

---
